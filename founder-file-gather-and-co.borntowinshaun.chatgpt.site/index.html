01 / The idea

Why make
another event?

Started August 2026Waterloo RegionSocial experiences

Because finding something to do is one thing. Finding people to do it with is another.

Keira noticed that meeting new people as an adult can be surprisingly hard. Plenty of local activities exist, but many feel easier when you already have someone to bring.

She started Gather & Co. in August 2026 to make events that work both ways: bring a friend, bring a group, or come alone and still have a way into the conversation.

“I wanted to create something where people could come alone without feeling awkward, but where people who came with friends would still have a great time too.”Keira · Email interview

03 / The hard part

Getting people
to take a chance.

At the start, Keira was asking people to buy a ticket to an event nobody had been to yet.

There were no Gather & Co. photos, past attendees, or reviews to point to. Ticket sales moved slowly, and she wondered whether the idea would work.

She reached out to people herself, followed up, worked with local creators and businesses, contacted media, and kept showing what the event could be. Eventually, the first event sold out.

05 / Behind Gather & Co.

Meet Keira.

Born in Kitchener, Keira O’Neill has lived in Cambridge for about ten years. She’s always liked the creative side of business: planning, marketing, connecting with people, and making an idea real.

She didn’t study business or entrepreneurship in college or university. Most of her learning has come through work, research, asking questions, and trying things out.

She’s also been involved with projects in real estate, land development, consulting, and construction. Those roles gave her experience with communication, organizing, marketing, clients, and solving problems—all useful for building Gather & Co.

06 / The impact so far

The part that
stayed with her.

Some guests were nervous about coming alone. Afterwards, they told Keira they were glad they came.

People who hadn’t met before were talking and laughing together. Some asked about the next event almost immediately. For Keira, that mattered more than painting pumpkins: it showed that people enjoyed being there with one another.

She’s careful about calling this a huge community impact after just one event. Her hope is to keep making those small connections and to bring more local businesses into future events through collaborations, prizes, food, products, and services.

✳Gather & Co. is still new. Keira described positive responses from guests after the first sold-out event.

08 / Looking ahead

Start now.
Keep learning.

“You don’t need to have everything figured out before you start.”

Keira learned more from hosting one event than she could have from months of planning. Some things cost more than expected; others worked better than she thought they would. She wants to use what she learned to make the next event better.

Her goal is to host different experiences regularly across the Waterloo Region, build stronger partnerships with local businesses, and eventually grow beyond the region. She wants people to see a Gather & Co. event and feel confident they can have a good time—even if they don’t know anyone else going.
